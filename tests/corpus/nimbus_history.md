# Nimbus Cloud: The Official History (2024)

Nimbus Cloud was founded in 2010 in Hangzhou by Ada Chen and Bo Liu.

The Series C round closed in 2015 at US$60 million, led by Harbor Ventures.

In 2019, the flagship platform launched after two years of trials.

Ada Chen said at the launch: "We build infrastructure for people, not for quarters."

In 2021, Nimbus reported revenue of US$120 million, more than doubling the previous year.

Bo Liu often said, "Infrastructure compounds; features don't."

邹涛说：“我们是自己给自己戴上了手铐。”

The company listed in 2023; the IPO raised HK$900 million and valued Nimbus at about US$3 billion.
