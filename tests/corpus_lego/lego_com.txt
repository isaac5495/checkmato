The LEGO Group was founded in 1932 by Ole Kirk Kristiansen.
The company has since passed from generation to generation and is to this day still owned by the Kirk Kristiansen family.
The brick in its present form was launched in 1958.
The beginning of the LEGO Group starts with wooden toys, such as cars, airplanes and yoyo's.
