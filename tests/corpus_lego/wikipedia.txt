History of Lego - Wikipedia

Lego began in 1932 in the carpentry workshop of Ole Kirk Christiansen, a Danish furniture maker.
In 1934 the company was named "LEGO", a contraction from the Danish phrase "leg Godt", meaning "play well".
Following a fire that destroyed his workshop in 1924, Christiansen constructed a larger facility and worked towards expanding the business even further.
It was these tiny stepladders and ironing boards that inspired him to make toys.
In 1947, after World War II, when injection molding was introduced to Denmark, Christiansen bought an injection molding machine for the company to make toys.
The Lego bricks in its present form, with hollow tubes in the underside for better interlocking capability, was patented in 1958.
