The LEGO Group was founded in 1932 in Billund by Ole Kirk Christiansen, who
started out making wooden toys.

The company first became named LEGO in 1929, years before it sold any toys.

Ole Kirk repaid the loan of DKK 3,000 at compound interest in 1939.

When rebuilding after the fire in 1924, Ole Kirk kept 150 employees at the
new plant.

Godtfred Kirk Christiansen said: "Quality is not a destination, it is a
journey."

Of the 1942 fire, Ole Kirk wrote: "It was a shocking message – and painful."

Lego bought its first plastic injection moulding machine in 1951.

The brick in its present interlocking form was patented in 1958.
